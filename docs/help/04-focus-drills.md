title: Focus drills and scores
tags: [focus, drill, score, level, concentration, number]
---

The focus drill shows a number for a moment, hides it, and asks you to type it back. Each correct answer raises your level and your streak; a wrong answer resets the streak.

Your focus score, high score, level, and accuracy are tracked as you play, and accuracy is calculated from your total correct answers against total attempts.

Drills get harder as your level rises by showing longer numbers for less time.
