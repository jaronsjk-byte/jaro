Word jumble scrambles a word and asks you to unscramble it. Solve it to raise your score and streak, then move to the next word.

If you are stuck, ask for a hint — ChillMind gives you a clue about the word rather than the answer itself.

Levels increase as you solve more words, and longer words appear at higher levels.
