Open **Breathing** from the sidebar to run a guided breathing session.

Pick a pattern, press start, and follow the on-screen animation — inhale as it grows, exhale as it shrinks. A session takes a couple of minutes.

Each completed session is added to your breathing history and counts towards your breathing streak. If you stop partway through, the session is not counted.
