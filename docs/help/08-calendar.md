title: Calendar and schedule
tags: [calendar, schedule, event, date, month, plan]
---

Open **Calendar** from the sidebar to see your month. Select any date to see what you have scheduled.

To add something, choose the date, then add a schedule item with a time, an activity name, and an icon. Items appear under the date you selected.

You can switch between month and day views from the controls at the top of the Calendar page.
