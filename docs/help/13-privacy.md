title: Your data and privacy
tags: [privacy, data, delete, stored, personal, security]
---

ChillMind keeps your wellbeing data — breathing history, scores, goals, schedule items, and chat — for the current session only. Nothing is written to a permanent database, and refreshing the page clears it.

Because nothing is stored permanently, there is no separate delete-my-data step: ending your session removes it.

Your conversation with the Mentor is not shared with other users.
