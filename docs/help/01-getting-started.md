title: Signing in and getting started
tags: [login, sign in, account, start, first time]
---

ChillMind opens on the sign-in screen. Enter your name to start a session, and you go straight to your dashboard.

The first time you sign in, ChillMind runs a short wellness survey so it can tailor your dashboard. You can skip it and take it later from your profile.

Your session lasts as long as the app is open. Refreshing the page starts a new session, so finish anything in progress before you reload.
