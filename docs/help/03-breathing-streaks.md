id: breathing-streaks
title: Breathing streaks and history
tags: [streak, history, reset, days, consecutive]
---

Your breathing streak counts consecutive days with at least one completed breathing session. Miss a day and the streak returns to zero on your next session.

Your breathing history is shown on the Breathing page and summarised on your profile.

There is no manual reset button for a streak. Because ChillMind keeps your progress for the current session only, refreshing the page clears your streak and history along with the rest of your session data.
