The Mentor is the chat on your dashboard. Ask about study techniques, focus, motivation, stress, or mindfulness and it replies in the conversation.

The Mentor keeps the recent part of your conversation in mind, so you can ask follow-up questions without repeating yourself.

The Mentor is a supportive study companion, not a counsellor or a doctor. It does not diagnose anything and does not give medical or medication advice.
